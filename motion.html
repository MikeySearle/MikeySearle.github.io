02Scroll with weight

The page arrives a beat after the wheel stops.

The document is a fixed sheet moved by hand each frame, chasing the real scroll position rather than matching it. The lag is the whole effect — and the same number, the velocity, drives the shear on the frames below and the direction of the band beneath them.
